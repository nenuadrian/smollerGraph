## Context

What forced a choice, and the episode it came up in. The problem as it stood then, with what was
measured, and where (an episode, a step, `bench/`). No more than the reader needs to see why it
mattered.

## Decision

What we do, in the present tense, as plainly as the code would say it. Name the files, the
settings and the numbers, so that someone can find it in the repo. If the reason for the choice
was never given, say so, rather than supply one after the fact.

## Alternatives considered

- Each one that was weighed, and why it lost. An obvious alternative that nobody weighed can go
  here too, marked as such, so a reader knows it wasn't overlooked by accident.

## Consequences

- What it costs, and what it makes easy.
- What bit later, and where, with the episode or the step.
